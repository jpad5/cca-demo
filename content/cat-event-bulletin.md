# CAT Event Bulletin - CAT-26-07

> Fictional bulletin for the Zava Insurance demo. Not real claims guidance.

**Event:** CAT-26-07, wildfire (fictional event)
**Loss dates:** August 10-20, 2026
**Applies to:** contents claims under homeowner and tenant policies

## Smoke-affected soft goods

- Bedding, textiles, rugs and clothing that keep a smoke odour after one professional cleaning may be replaced rather than cleaned.
- Record the cleaning attempt, or the reason it was skipped, in the item notes.

## Electronics

- Electronics exposed to heat or heavy smoke may be replaced without a repair estimate when the replacement cost is under CAD 500.
- Above CAD 500, record the failure symptom, for example "does not power on".

## Specialty items

- Jewelry, art, collectibles and antiques go to specialist appraisal. Don't value them from retail listings.

## Depreciation

- Use the approved rule set for this event, Zava Demo Depreciation Rules v1.0. Adjusters don't change rates on individual items.

## Documentation

- Keep the original inventory file unchanged. Make corrections on the item record, with a reason.
